# CFOP: after the first full solve

Teach these only once the learner can solve the cube with 2-look OLL and PLL.

1. **Plan the cross before turning.** Use the inspection time to find all four cross edges and plan 6-8 moves without looking at the cube while turning.
2. **F2L without rotations.** Insert back slots with `R'`/`L` versions (`R' U R`, `L U' L'`) instead of turning the whole cube; it saves time.
3. **Look ahead.** While doing one F2L pair, find the next one. Turn slower so your eyes can move ahead.
4. **Full OLL and PLL.** 57 + 21 algorithms let you finish each in one look. Learn PLL first (fewer, more common), starting with the cases that 2-look PLL needs twice.
5. **Fingertricks.** Turn `U` with the index finger and `R` with the wrist; avoid regrips.

Typical progression: about 2 minutes with LBL, under 1 minute with 2-look CFOP, under 30 seconds with full CFOP and look-ahead.
